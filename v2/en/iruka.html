Weight budget and buoyancy

On an electric jet ski the battery is heavy, so every kilo has to sit in the right place. The weight budget adds up hull, battery, motor and crew, then the waterline is checked on the 3D model with one to three people aboard, before the mould is made.

Why it matters: a hull that carries its weight correctly gets on the plane sooner, wets less surface and uses less energy. This is where the first kWh of range are won.
