FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a licence to ride IRUKA?

In France, a boat licence is required to ride a jet ski of this power. Without one, your trial is done with an E-Dolphin pilot.

How is IRUKA charged?

Through a CCS2 socket, the same as electric cars: about 30 minutes on a DC fast charger. On-board charging is studied with each yacht crew.

Where can I ride?

Wherever jet skis are allowed, like a combustion jet ski. Some waters ban them whatever the energy, such as Lake Annecy: there we only offer quayside presentations.

How does the deposit work?

Every model is reserved with a 30% deposit on the total price, options included: €10,797 excl. VAT for an IRUKA without options, $27,000 excl. VAT for a Launch Edition. The configurator works out the exact deposit for your build. It will be payable online by secure payment once it opens; your request already holds your place.

When will I receive it?

First deliveries are targeted for spring 2028, starting with the Launch Edition. Your date is confirmed by our team when you reserve.

What about maintenance?

An electric motor needs no oil change, no spark plugs and no fuel to stabilise in winter. Battery monitoring and updates are done remotely.
